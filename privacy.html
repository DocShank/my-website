Your Only Doc Autopilot

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Purpose

Your Only Doc Autopilot is a private desktop application used by the owner of the Your Only Doc Podcast to prepare and upload approved podcast videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube data

The application uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to obtain the authorization needed to upload and manage podcast videos on the owner's channel. It does not request access for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials, upload records, scripts, audio, and videos are stored locally on the owner's personal Mac. The application does not sell Google user data and does not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Retention and deletion

Local authorization information is retained only while the owner uses the automation. The owner can delete the local credentials at any time and can revoke access through the Google Account permissions page.

Google API policy

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Access is limited to the owner's device and Google account. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the application is designed to minimize access and keep credentials local.
