Data & scientific note

This development build uses the MNI152 T1 MRI template with an AAL atlas label map. Atlas background does not mean “no anatomy”; it means that voxel is not parcellated by this particular atlas. The long-term platform will combine complementary atlases rather than inventing names where an atlas has no label.
