Your Only Doc Autopilot

Your Only Doc Autopilot is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the Your Only Doc Podcast. It prepares reviewed medical podcast videos and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload approved episodes to the owner's YouTube channel.

There is no public registration, advertising service, or sale of user information. The authorization credentials and upload records remain on the owner's personal Mac.
